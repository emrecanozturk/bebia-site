Questions

How do I add someone to my family?

Create an invite link in the Family tab and share it. The person who joins sees and adds entries with the access you give them.

Does Bebia suggest doses?

No. You enter the dose from your doctor's prescription; Bebia never diagnoses. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Which devices does it run on?

Bebia runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. The Apple Watch app works together with Bebia on your iPhone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Bebia. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period.

How do I export my data?

In the app: Settings › Privacy Center › Export your data. The file is prepared on your phone.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Privacy Center › Delete account. The request waits 30 days, and you can cancel it during that time.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Log nursing, bottles, diapers and sleep with one tap on your watch, and see nursing and sleep timers on your wrist. The watch app works together with Bebia on your iPhone.

Can I use it on iPad?

Yes. On iPad, Bebia uses the big screen: list and entry side by side. Sign in with the same account and your family sees the same log.
