Privacy Policy

On this page

  • What we process
  • Why
  • Who receives data
  • How long
  • Your rights

This page is a translation for your convenience. If anything differs from the English version, the English version applies. English

Bebia (“we”) is operated by Emrecan Öztürk, Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: support@bebia.app. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

What we process

  • Account: your email address or Sign in with Apple identifier, sign-in records, and your consent choices with their dates.
  • Family: family members' names, roles and permissions; for members who log via WhatsApp (Türkiye only), their phone number. The number is visible only to the family admin and the number's owner; it is deleted if the owner does not reply “Evet” within 14 days or writes “dur”.
  • Baby care records: feeds, sleep, diapers, temperature, medicine given, vaccine dates, growth measurements and notes, and who entered each record.
  • Health profile: medicines (you enter the dose from the prescription), vaccine contents, allergies, chronic conditions and the emergency card. Vaccine contents, allergies, chronic conditions, the emergency card, notes typed in the app, symptom names and baby photos are end-to-end encrypted with your family key; our server cannot read them. Medicine lists, doses given, temperature, feeding, sleep and diaper records are not end-to-end encrypted so that double-dose and red-flag rules can also run on the server.
  • Parent sleep (optional, with Apple Health permission): only the nightly total sleep minutes; bedtimes are not sent. Only you, the other parent and the family admin can see it.
  • Device: notification tokens, an install identifier, language, time zone, unit preference, app version, notification preferences and a public key used to encrypt notification details.
  • Subscription: plan status and expiry date. Payment details stay with Apple.

We do not store WhatsApp message text, do not collect location or advertising identifiers, and do not track you.

Why

To provide the service and share it within your family (contract), and to process your baby's health data with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw in the app's Privacy Center. AI summaries are written on your device; names, allergies and conditions are never sent to an AI model. Safety notifications (double dose, red flags) are part of the service.

Who receives data

  • Supabase (servers in the European Union, Frankfurt).
  • Apple (push notifications with encrypted details, Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions).
  • Resend (sign-in code email: your email address and a one-time code).
  • Meta WhatsApp Business, only if a family member logs via WhatsApp (their messages and our replies).

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

How long

While your account is active. If you delete your account, the request waits 30 days (you can cancel), then your personal data is permanently deleted. Deleted records are purged after 30 days; the notification queue after 7 days; WhatsApp message identifiers after 2 days and delivery errors after 7 days; the audit log after 2 years. A salted hash of your identifier is kept as proof of deletion for as long as legally required; subscription records for the period required by commercial law.

Your rights

You can access, export (Privacy Center › Export your data), correct and delete your data, and object to processing. Write to support@bebia.app. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
